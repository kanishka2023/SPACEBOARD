How to play

1. Aim before you pinch

Point your middle fingertip at any bar — each one is a note (S R G m P D N...). That's the note you'll play.

2. Pinch to play

Touch your index and middle fingertip together to sound the note you're aiming at. Release to stop.

Move to a new bar while still pinching, and it retriggers — the new note takes over right away.

3. Up is louder, down is softer

Move your pinched fingertip up or down to control volume — this works all the time, even before you pinch, so you can set your level first.

4. Two hands, two notes

Each hand plays and controls volume independently — hold a note in each hand at once.

Customize

Octave range (L / U)

Toggle the Lower and Upper octave rows on or off from the left panel. At least one must stay on.

Transpose

Shift every note up or down in semitones upto:(-6)/(+6). Base Scale: C

Filter your notes

Tap any bar to tuck it away into the side box if you don't need it. Tap it again in the box to bring it back — handy for practicing with just a few notes, like S R G.

First time? Start simple. Hide everything except S R G, pinch to sound one, and get a feel for volume with your fingertip height before turning octaves and transpose back on.

Why Customization?

Octave range (L / U)

Not every piece needs both octaves. Pick Lower or Upper to keep your workspace focused on the range you're actually playing in.

Transpose

Shift every note by semitones, −6 to +6 — the full range of Western transposition. Play any composition in the key it needs, with the same finger positions.

Filter notes

Omit any note your composition never uses to unclutter the screen — this removes it from both octaves at once. Helps most when staying specific to a Raga.
